# ADR-005: Affiliate Economics Assumptions, Provenance, Numeric Semantics, and Calculation Authority

## Status
Proposed

## Date
2026-10-06

## Context
In Phase 6 of the TikTok Shop Affiliate Trend Hunter, we introduce affiliate economics and profitability intelligence to support creator and affiliate decision-making.

Previous phases established:
- Phase 1–2: Market trend ingestion, snapshot tracking, orthogonal evidence signals, and the deterministic Trend Score.
- Phase 3: Curated Watchlists, durable post-commit alert evaluation, and synchronous startup catch-up (ADR-003).
- Phase 4: Hot SQLite backup, offline launcher-owned restore, keyset streaming CSV exports, and compatibility versioning (ADR-004).
- Phase 5: Opportunity workspace, Collections decision management, transient Product Compare, and strict Saved Views.

Research conducted for Phase 6 (`research/PHASE_6_AFFILIATE_ECONOMICS_RESEARCH.md`, accepted under commit `8c3f8949b8c9e61691c2e2756557e9abadc035b5`) investigated whether affiliate economics can be automatically derived from market data, crawler observations, or official TikTok APIs.

Key findings and architectural tensions:
1. **Attribution Disconnect**: Public market demand (Product cumulative sold count and 24-hour sales velocity) reflects general market volume across all sellers and creators. Affiliate compensation is attribution-based: creators earn commission exclusively on orders generated through their own affiliate showcase, video links, or live sessions. Presenting market sales velocity multiplied by commission as affiliate earnings is factually untrue and commercially misleading.
2. **Context-Dependent Commission**: Official TikTok Shop Vietnam documentation and field realities show that commission rates depend heavily on collaboration type (Open Collaboration vs. Target Collaboration vs. Shop Ads), creator tier, creator-seller negotiation, time-bounded campaigns, and post-order adjustments (refunds, cancellations, voucher deductions, tax withholding). The crawler-observed `ProductSnapshot.commission_rate` field represents an unauthenticated, public snapshot whose creator context and payout authority cannot be guaranteed.
3. **External Integration Complexity vs. Local Desktop Core**: An official TikTok Shop Partner/Affiliate API integration requires Partner approval, OAuth authorization, secure token persistence/refresh, external network availability, and intricate reconciliation between platform and user data. Scraped Creator Center dashboards would require private session storage, CAPTCHA bypass, and high maintenance fragility.
4. **Numeric Representation & Financial Consistency**: Legacy product snapshots store price and commission rate as floating-point numbers (`Float`), which can introduce binary rounding artifacts. The application runs locally on SQLite in Vietnam (`VND`), where currency has no fractional sub-units and users think in whole VND and percentage or basis points.
5. **Architectural Separation**: Financial evaluation must not pollute the objective, algorithmic assessment of market trends, nor distort Watchlists, alert rules, or discovery crawling.

To ensure integrity, transparency, and architectural stability, Phase 6 requires an explicit architecture decision freezing domain boundaries, source strategy, ownership, default semantics, numeric representations, calculation authority, and backup compatibility.

## Decision

### 1. Domain Separation
The architecture strictly enforces separation across three distinct domains:

1. **Market Evidence Domain**:
   - Observed Product price, cumulative sold count, 24-hour sales velocity, review count, rating score.
   - Trend Score, trend momentum, orthogonal evidence confidence, Why Trending explanations.
   - Crawler-observed snapshot fields (including raw observed commission rate).
   - *Semantics*: Objective, algorithmic observations of external market state.

2. **User Economics Assumptions Domain**:
   - Assumed affiliate commission rate (`commission_rate_bps`).
   - Assumed commission base per order (`commission_base_vnd`), provenance, and observation timestamp.
   - Fixed campaign cost assumptions: sample acquisition cost, content production cost, promotion/ad spend.
   - Variable cost assumption: operating/fulfillment cost per attributable order.
   - Optional expected attributable order volume.
   - *Semantics*: Explicit, user-defined business scenario parameters.

3. **Derived Economics Domain**:
   - Estimated commission per attributable order.
   - Total fixed campaign cost.
   - Contribution margin per attributable order.
   - Break-even attributable order count.
   - Optional expected gross commission, expected net contribution, and return on cost.
   - *Semantics*: Deterministic, read-time calculations based strictly on scenario assumptions.

**Isolation Invariant**:
Economics assumptions and derived metrics must NEVER alter or influence:
- Trend Score calculation or weighting;
- Trend eligibility, signals, or velocity classifications;
- Evidence confidence or Why Trending diagnostic output;
- Watchlist membership or monitoring lifecycle;
- Alert rule evaluation or trigger thresholds (ADR-003);
- Crawler execution, scheduling, or discovery prioritization.

There shall be no blended "trend-profit score" or composite ranking combining market velocity with hypothetical profitability.

### 2. Attribution Prohibition
The architecture establishes an absolute invariant:

> **Product sold count and market sales velocity are market evidence only. They MUST NOT be used to infer user attributable orders, affiliate earnings, affiliate revenue, creator conversion rate, or investment payback period.**

Specifically, the system explicitly prohibits:
- Any formula equivalent to `sales_velocity × commission_per_order` presented as creator/affiliate earnings;
- Any projection of cumulative `sold_count × commission_per_order` presented as historical affiliate revenue;
- Calculating payback time by dividing fixed campaign costs by market sales velocity;
- Fabricating creator conversion rates or click-through rates from market data.

All affiliate economics calculations require either:
1. Explicit user-entered attributable order assumptions (`expected_attributed_orders`); or
2. A future authoritative creator-attribution source approved under a separate architecture decision.

### 3. Manual-First Source Strategy
For the Phase 6 MVP:
- Affiliate commission rate is strictly a user scenario assumption.
- Commission base amount is strictly a user scenario assumption.
- The current observed Product price may optionally be copied into the scenario as a labeled, frozen proxy (`observed_price_proxy`).
- The existing `ProductSnapshot.commission_rate` field is NOT an automatic calculation authority.
- No private TikTok Creator Center scraping is permitted.
- No official TikTok Affiliate API or OAuth integration is included in the MVP.
- No silent background refresh of scenario financial assumptions from crawler runs.
- No silent overwrite of user-entered numbers under any circumstances.

Future official Affiliate API integration is classified as conditional future work requiring separate architecture review covering credentials, security boundaries, and explicit data-source precedence.

### 4. Scenario Ownership and Cardinality
The data model defines:
```
Product 1 ───< 0..* EconomicsScenario
```
- Each `EconomicsScenario` belongs directly to a single `Product`.
- Multiple scenarios per Product are supported to allow comparing organic vs. paid campaigns, varying commission tiers, or conservative vs. aggressive cost models.
- `EconomicsScenario` is architecturally decoupled from:
  - Product market state (`products`, `product_snapshots`);
  - Watchlist monitoring state (`watchlist_items`);
  - Collection organization (`collections`, `collection_items`).
- Collections remain strictly decision organization containers. Structured financial assumptions must NOT be stored in `CollectionItem.notes`.
- There is no persistent `CollectionItem → EconomicsScenario` foreign key in the MVP; Collection views reference the Product's default scenario.

### 5. Default Scenario Semantics and Invariants
To ensure unambiguous decision support in Collection and Compare views, scenario defaults follow deterministic rules:
- **Zero Scenarios**: A Product with 0 scenarios has 0 default scenarios.
- **One or More Scenarios**: A Product with $\ge 1$ scenarios must have **exactly one** committed default scenario.
- **Initial Scenario**: The first scenario created for a Product is automatically committed as default (`is_default = true`).
- **Atomic Default Switching**: Setting a scenario as default is an atomic operation:
  1. Clear `is_default = false` across all scenarios for that `product_id`;
  2. Set `is_default = true` on the target scenario;
  3. Commit within a single database transaction.
- **Database Partial Uniqueness**: The database schema enforces at most one default per product where supported by SQLite:
  ```sql
  CREATE UNIQUE INDEX uq_economics_scenarios_product_default
  ON economics_scenarios(product_id)
  WHERE is_default = 1;
  ```
- **Deterministic Promotion on Deletion**:
  - Deleting a non-default scenario preserves the existing default.
  - Deleting the sole scenario leaves the Product with 0 scenarios and 0 defaults.
  - Deleting the current default when other scenarios remain MUST atomically promote the oldest remaining scenario ordered by `(created_at ASC, id ASC)`.
- **Concurrency & Transactionality**: Creation, update, default-switching, and deletion must execute inside explicit transactional boundaries to preserve the exactly-one-default invariant without race conditions.

### 6. VND-Only Currency Scope
Phase 6 MVP is strictly **VND-only**:
- All monetary inputs, calculations, and display outputs operate in whole Vietnamese Đồng (VND).
- No generic multi-currency data structures, currency configuration tables, or ISO currency columns in the MVP scenario table.
- No foreign exchange (FX) conversion engine.
- Observed Product price may be utilized as a proxy only because repository ingestion currently accepts explicit VND prices (non-VND prices are parsed as null).
- Multi-currency support is deferred and requires a dedicated future architectural design.

### 7. Persisted Numeric Semantics
Financial assumptions are stored using exact integers:
- **Monetary Amounts**: Persisted as whole `INTEGER` VND (`commission_base_vnd`, `sample_cost_vnd`, `content_cost_vnd`, `promotion_cost_vnd`, `variable_cost_per_attributed_order_vnd`).
- **Commission Rate**: Persisted as whole `INTEGER` basis points (`commission_rate_bps`), where:
  $$10,000 \text{ bps} = 100.00\% \quad (100 \text{ bps} = 1.00\%)$$
  Bounded domain: $0 \le \text{commission\_rate\_bps} \le 10,000$.
- **Expected Orders**: Persisted as nullable `INTEGER` ($0 \le \text{expected\_attributed\_orders}$).

**Rationale & Trade-Offs**:
- Eliminates floating-point imprecision and binary rounding artifacts inherent in SQLite `REAL` / Python `float`.
- VND has no fractional sub-units in practical commerce; integer representation is natural and lossless.
- Avoids the operational and serialization complexity of Decimal/Numeric adapters across SQLite, SQLAlchemy, Pydantic, and Next.js / TypeScript.
- Basis points provide exact integer representations for all common commission rates down to two decimal places (e.g., $12.5\% = 1,250 \text{ bps}$).

### 8. Null versus Zero Semantics
The architecture enforces strict semantic distinction between missing information and intentional zero:
- `null`: Unknown, omitted, or unstated assumption.
- `0`: Explicit user assertion that the cost or rate is zero or not applicable (e.g., free sample, no paid promotion, zero variable handling cost).
- Input fields must NEVER be silently coerced from `null` to `0`.
- If an upstream input required by a formula is `null`, the downstream derived metric MUST evaluate to `null`. It must not fabricate zero cost or infinite margin.

### 9. Fixed versus Variable Cost Semantics
Campaign expenditures are strictly categorized:
- **Fixed Campaign Costs**: One-time or period investments required to launch/run the affiliate campaign:
  - Sample acquisition cost (`sample_cost_vnd`);
  - Content production cost (`content_cost_vnd`);
  - Promotion / paid ad spend (`promotion_cost_vnd`).
- **Variable Operating Cost**: Incremental cost incurred per attributable order:
  - Packaging, handling, support, or extra affiliate fulfillment fees (`variable_cost_per_attributed_order_vnd`).

**Rule**: Fixed campaign costs are never subtracted from individual unit order economics. They are amortized across campaign volume via the break-even calculation.

### 10. Backend Calculation Authority
All economics calculations must proceed through a single, authoritative backend engine:
- **Architecture Boundary**:
  - Pure calculation module (`EconomicsCalculator`): Deterministic, side-effect-free formulas executing exact integer/rational arithmetic.
  - Scenario service (`EconomicsService`): Persistence, CRUD, transaction management, default scenario invariant enforcement, and snapshot price freezing.
  - API endpoints: Return normalized assumptions alongside calculated derived results.
  - Frontend client: Presentation, input capture, and currency formatting only.
- **No Independent Frontend Formulas**: The frontend must never implement independent financial formulas or rounding rules.
- **Provisional Previews**: If interactive client-side preview is introduced in the future, it must be explicitly labeled as provisional and reconciled against backend authority upon submission.

### 11. Rounding and Precision Authority
- **Persisted Assumptions**: Exact integers and basis points; no rounding applied during storage.
- **Intermediate Arithmetic**: Rational calculation maintaining exact integer fractions:
  $$\text{RATE\_SCALE} = 10,000$$
- **Monetary Output Rounding**: Derived monetary values returned via API or rendered in UI use a single backend policy: **round-half-up to nearest whole VND** (`round_half_up_vnd`).
- **Unit Contribution in Break-Even**: Break-even calculations must use the exact rational contribution margin numerator, avoiding off-by-one errors caused by premature rounding of per-order contribution.

### 12. Authoritative Formula Semantics
All derived metrics are defined deterministically:

1. **Estimated Commission per Attributable Order**:
   $$\text{commission\_per\_order\_vnd} = \operatorname{round}\left( \frac{\text{commission\_base\_vnd} \times \text{commission\_rate\_bps}}{10,000} \right)$$
   *Prerequisites*: Requires non-null `commission_base_vnd` and `commission_rate_bps`; otherwise `null`.

2. **Total Fixed Campaign Cost**:
   $$\text{fixed\_campaign\_cost\_vnd} = \text{sample\_cost\_vnd} + \text{content\_cost\_vnd} + \text{promotion\_cost\_vnd}$$
   *Prerequisites*: Evaluated only when all three components are known (non-null or explicitly 0); otherwise `null`.

3. **Contribution Margin per Attributable Order**:
   $$\text{contribution\_per\_order\_vnd} = \text{commission\_per\_order\_vnd} - \text{variable\_cost\_per\_attributed\_order\_vnd}$$
   *Prerequisites*: Requires calculated commission per order and known variable cost; otherwise `null`.

4. **Break-Even Attributable Orders**:
   Let the exact contribution numerator be:
   $$\text{contrib\_num} = (\text{commission\_base\_vnd} \times \text{commission\_rate\_bps}) - (\text{variable\_cost\_per\_attributed\_order\_vnd} \times 10,000)$$
   If `fixed_campaign_cost_vnd` is known, $\text{contrib\_num} > 0$, and $\text{fixed\_campaign\_cost\_vnd} \ge 0$:
   $$\text{break\_even\_orders} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{fixed\_campaign\_cost\_vnd} \times 10,000}{\text{contrib\_num}} \right\rceil$$
   If $\text{contrib\_num} \le 0$ (negative or zero contribution margin) or inputs are incomplete, `break_even_orders` is `null`.

5. **Expected Campaign Metrics** (evaluated only when `expected_attributed_orders` is non-null):
   - **Expected Gross Commission**:
     $$\text{expected\_commission\_vnd} = \operatorname{round}\left( \frac{\text{commission\_base\_vnd} \times \text{commission\_rate\_bps} \times \text{expected\_attributed\_orders}}{10,000} \right)$$
   - **Expected Variable Cost**:
     $$\text{expected\_variable\_cost\_vnd} = \text{variable\_cost\_per\_attributed\_order\_vnd} \times \text{expected\_attributed\_orders}$$
   - **Expected Net Contribution**:
     $$\text{expected\_contribution\_vnd} = \text{expected\_commission\_vnd} - \text{fixed\_campaign\_cost\_vnd} - \text{expected\_variable\_cost\_vnd}$$
     *(Evaluated only when all components are known).*
   - **Scenario Return on Cost**:
     Let $\text{total\_campaign\_cost} = \text{fixed\_campaign\_cost\_vnd} + \text{expected\_variable\_cost\_vnd}$.
     If $\text{total\_campaign\_cost} > 0$ and `expected_contribution_vnd` is known:
     $$\text{return\_on\_cost\_ratio} = \frac{\text{expected\_contribution\_vnd}}{\text{total\_campaign\_cost}}$$
     Returned as a transparent decimal ratio / percentage. Must never be labeled as guaranteed commercial ROI.

### 13. Derived-Result Persistence Policy
- All derived economics metrics are **computed on read**.
- The database does NOT persist calculated commission, contribution, break-even orders, expected net contribution, or return on cost.
- No denormalized calculation-result tables, cached columns, or materialized result views in the MVP.
- Prevents cache invalidation drift and guarantees that formula refinements or bug fixes immediately apply across all scenarios without database migrations.

### 14. Frozen Observed-Price Proxy Semantics
When a user chooses to base their scenario on the currently observed Product price:
1. The backend copies the latest available snapshot price (`analysis.deltas.latest_price`).
2. The value is validated, rounded/converted to whole integer VND, and assigned to `commission_base_vnd`.
3. Provenance is explicitly tagged: `commission_base_source = 'observed_price_proxy'`.
4. The exact observation timestamp is frozen: `commission_base_observed_at = snapshot.captured_at`.
5. Subsequent crawler runs, price changes, or promotions do NOT automatically alter the scenario's commission base.
6. Refreshing or rebasing the price is strictly an explicit user action.
7. The UI must clearly label this value as a frozen proxy, not the official TikTok commission payout base.

### 15. Persistence Scope and Minimal Schema Principle
Phase 6 MVP introduces **exactly one** new persistent database table:
- Table name: `economics_scenarios`
- Conceptual attributes:
  - `id`: Integer primary key;
  - `product_id`: Integer foreign key referencing `products.id` (ON DELETE CASCADE);
  - `name`: String scenario label (unique per product);
  - `commission_rate_bps`: Nullable integer basis points ($0 \dots 10,000$);
  - `commission_base_vnd`: Nullable integer VND;
  - `commission_base_source`: String enum (`user_entered`, `observed_price_proxy`);
  - `commission_base_observed_at`: Nullable UTC datetime;
  - `sample_cost_vnd`: Nullable integer VND;
  - `content_cost_vnd`: Nullable integer VND;
  - `promotion_cost_vnd`: Nullable integer VND;
  - `variable_cost_per_attributed_order_vnd`: Nullable integer VND;
  - `expected_attributed_orders`: Nullable integer;
  - `is_default`: Boolean default indicator;
  - `created_at`: UTC datetime;
  - `updated_at`: UTC datetime.
- Indexes:
  - Standard foreign-key index on `product_id`;
  - Unique composite index on `(product_id, name)`;
  - Unique partial index on `product_id` where `is_default = 1`.

**Minimal-Table Principle**:
The architecture rejects additional tables in Phase 6 for:
- Granular expense line items;
- Immutable assumption revision history;
- Crawler commission observations;
- Custom formula definitions;
- Persisted calculation outputs.

### 16. Backup Compatibility Versioning
Under ADR-004's established backup and restore integrity policy:
- Adding the required `economics_scenarios` table modifies the application's required relational schema derived dynamically from `Base.metadata`.
- Application startup advances the durable compatibility marker:
  $$\text{portable\_backup\_compatibility\_version: } \mathbf{"2" \longrightarrow "3"}$$
- **Compatibility Invariants**:
  - ADR-004's **same-version restore policy remains strictly authoritative**.
  - Backups created under version `"2"` (Phase 5) will be rejected by the Phase 6 (version `"3"`) application upon restore candidate validation.
  - No cross-version database migrations are supported in Phase 6.
  - Schema upgrades on local installations occur via additive SQLAlchemy `create_all()` plus bootstrap verification.
  - The compatibility marker advances only after verified schema creation.
  - Operators must generate a fresh version `"3"` backup following successful Phase 6 upgrade.
  - Upgrade, backup rejection, and restore round-trip tests must execute against isolated, file-backed SQLite instances.

### 17. Compare and Collections Query Boundaries
Economics integration must adhere to strict query budgets to prevent N+1 regressions:
- **Collections Workspace**:
  - Displays default scenario summary (commission/order, contribution/order, break-even orders) alongside Collection member rows.
  - Fetched via a single batched default-scenario lookup for all displayed product IDs (`WHERE product_id IN (...) AND is_default = 1`).
  - Total Collection member-list candidate budget: **$\le 3$ SQL queries** (2 queries baseline + 1 batched economics query).
  - Zero per-row N+1 queries.
- **Product Compare Subsystem**:
  - Existing transient Compare workspace remains a unified subsystem; no separate "Economics Compare" tool.
  - Economics columns populated from each Product's default scenario. Products lacking scenarios render clean empty states.
  - Candidate query budget:
    - **$\le 3$ SQL queries** without Collection context (2 baseline + 1 batched economics);
    - **$\le 4$ SQL queries** with Collection context (3 baseline + 1 batched economics).
  - Zero per-product HTTP fanout.
- No composite economics ranking or sorting scores introduced into query plans.

### 18. Saved Views and Alert Rule Immutability
- **Saved Views**: Phase 5 Saved View V1 schemas (`market_trends`, `opportunity_workspace`) remain frozen and immutable. No economics filter keys or sort keys are added to V1 schemas in Phase 6 MVP.
- **Alert System**: Phase 3 alert evaluation rules (ADR-003) remain unchanged. No economics alert types (e.g., commission rate change, break-even crossing) are introduced in Phase 6 MVP.
- Any future expansion of Saved View schemas or alert conditions requires separate research and architecture review.

### 19. External Boundary and Security Posture
Phase 6 MVP is strictly **local-first**:
- All financial assumptions and scenario records are stored locally in the desktop SQLite database (`backend/affiliate.db`).
- No external economics APIs, external webhooks, or cloud telemetry.
- No storage of creator credentials, OAuth tokens, or sensitive financial account details.
- No background scraping of authenticated TikTok Creator Center sessions.
- No automated transmission of user financial models off the host machine.
- Backup exclusion guarantees (ADR-004) remain in effect: browser session cookies and tokens remain excluded from portable backups.

## Rejected Alternatives & Trade-Offs

| Alternative | Decision | Reason Rejected / Deferred |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Ingesting Crawler `commission_rate` as Truth** | **Rejected** | `ProductSnapshot.commission_rate` lacks creator context, collaboration type, tier awareness, and settlement guarantees. Silently treating it as truth produces misleading earnings figures. |
| **2. Private Creator Center Dashboard Scraping** | **Rejected** | Requires invasive authentication session capture, breaks desktop security boundaries, risks account compromise, and creates extreme anti-bot / CAPTCHA maintenance fragility. |
| **3. Official Affiliate API Integration for MVP** | **Deferred** | Requires TikTok Shop Partner approval, OAuth infrastructure, token rotation, network coupling, and complex precedence logic. Incompatible with minimum local-first MVP scope. |
| **4. Floating-Point Financial Persistence (`Float`)** | **Rejected** | Binary IEEE-754 floating point introduces inexact representations and rounding discrepancies. Inappropriate for financial contracts and user trust. |
| **5. SQL `DECIMAL` / `NUMERIC` Column Types** | **Rejected** | SQLite lacks native fixed-point Decimal types (uses dynamic type affinity). Whole VND + basis points stored as `INTEGER` provides exact, native, and simpler storage without custom serialization layers. |
| **6. Storing Economics on Product or CollectionItem** | **Rejected** | Financial assumptions are user-specific scenarios, not intrinsic product attributes. Multiple scenarios per product are necessary, and CollectionItem is purely an organizational container. |
| **7. Single Scenario Only per Product** | **Rejected** | Affiliates frequently evaluate multiple strategies for one product (e.g., organic showcase vs. paid ad promotion vs. custom negotiated rate). A single scenario forces destructive overwrites. |
| **8. Persisting Pre-Calculated Results in Database** | **Rejected** | Denormalized calculations introduce data staleness, cache invalidation hazards, and require complex synchronization on updates or formula refinements. Read-time calculation is fast and robust. |
| **9. Economics CSV Export in MVP** | **Deferred** | Phase 4 CSV export architecture is capable of supporting scenarios, but file export is not critical to core scenario decision support. Deferred to prevent scope expansion. |
| **10. Economics Alerts in MVP** | **Deferred** | Since assumptions are user-entered and change only on manual action, automated background alerts (e.g., threshold crossing) offer little utility without an automated rate feed. |
| **11. Dedicated "Economics Compare" Subsystem** | **Rejected** | Duplicating the Compare engine fragments the user experience. Adding optional economics columns to the existing Phase 5 transient Compare workspace is unified and efficient. |

## Consequences

### Positive Consequences
- **Truthful Economics**: Prevents dangerous and deceptive claims of affiliate earnings based on market sales velocity.
- **Deterministic Financial Precision**: Integer VND and basis points eliminate binary floating-point artifacts and ensure exact, reproducible calculations across backend and frontend.
- **Local-First Privacy & Security**: Financial assumptions remain 100% private and host-bound with zero third-party dependencies or token storage risks.
- **Architectural Isolation**: Market trend algorithms, Trend Score, alert evaluators, and crawlers remain pristine and completely unaffected by subjective user finances.
- **Bounded System Complexity**: Exactly one new table (`economics_scenarios`), read-time calculations, and batched queries maintain SQLite performance and avoid schema sprawl.
- **Predictable Disaster Recovery**: Adherence to ADR-004 compatibility versioning (`"2" → "3"`) guarantees backup integrity and prevents silent data corruption across schema evolution.

### Costs & Trade-Offs
- **Manual Maintenance**: Users must manually enter or update commission rates and cost assumptions; changes on TikTok Shop are not automatically reflected.
- **Proxy Limitations**: When the user selects `observed_price_proxy`, the frozen snapshot price may diverge from actual buyer-paid prices due to platform vouchers, discounts, or refunds.
- **Backup Incompatibility**: In accordance with ADR-004's same-version policy, Phase 5 version `"2"` backups cannot be restored into the Phase 6 version `"3"` application.
- **Concurrency Care Required**: The exactly-one-default invariant requires transactional discipline during scenario creation, deletion, and default toggling to avoid race conditions.
- **Single Currency Restriction**: The application cannot model multi-currency or cross-border affiliate commerce without future architectural expansion.

---

## Deferred Future Work
The following capabilities are explicitly out of scope for ADR-005 and Phase 6 MVP:
- Official TikTok Shop Partner / Affiliate API integration with OAuth authorization;
- Automated ingestion of attributed creator orders and settlement reports;
- Historical commission rate tracking and trend analytics;
- Automated economics alerts (e.g., break-even shifts or platform rate drops);
- Multi-currency support and foreign exchange (FX) conversion engines;
- Taxation, VAT, withholding tax calculations, and formal accounting/bookkeeping;
- Seller-side economics (e.g., platform commissions, payment gateway fees, merchant fulfillment);
- Arbitrary user-defined formula and spreadsheet expression builders;
- AI-driven profitability optimization or automated campaign budget recommendations;
- Dedicated economics CSV export streams;
- Multi-user collaboration, permissions, or cloud database synchronization.

## Relationship to Existing ADRs
- **ADR-003 (`Durable Post-Commit Alert Evaluation and Replay Semantics`)**:
  Remains active and authoritative. Alert evaluation semantics, event uniqueness, condition edge crossings, and catch-up ordering are completely unaffected by Phase 6. No economics alerts are introduced.
- **ADR-004 (`Local Backup/Restore Integrity, Keyset Streaming CSV Exports, and Compatibility Semantics`)**:
  Remains active and authoritative for backup mechanics, staging validation, offline launcher-owned restore, and same-version policy. ADR-005 extends ADR-004 solely by establishing the necessity of advancing `portable_backup_compatibility_version` from `"2"` to `"3"` due to the introduction of the required `economics_scenarios` schema.
- **ADR-005**:
  Introduces the Phase 6 affiliate economics domain, freezing manual-first source semantics, Product-scenario ownership, integer numeric policies, deterministic formulas, and read-time calculation authority.
