# ADR-006: TikTok Content Intelligence & Video Signals Architecture

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-10-07

## Context
In Phase 7 of the TikTok Shop Affiliate Trend Hunter, we introduce content intelligence and video signals to fulfill the foundational product architecture set forth in [`Project.md`](../../Project.md) (Sections 1, 22–24):
```text
TikTok Shop Product Data (Phases 1–5)
        +
Affiliate Economics (Phase 6)
        +
TikTok Content Data (Phase 7)
        ↓
Composite Trend & Opportunity Intelligence
        ↓
"Which TikTok Shop products should I consider promoting as an affiliate today?"
```

Previous phases established:
- **Phases 1–2:** Market trend ingestion, snapshot tracking, orthogonal evidence signals, and the deterministic 0–100 `Trend Score`.
- **Phase 3:** Curated Watchlists, durable post-commit alert evaluation, and synchronous startup catch-up ([`ADR-003`](ADR-003.md)).
- **Phase 4:** Hot SQLite backup, offline launcher-owned restore, keyset streaming CSV exports, and compatibility versioning ([`ADR-004`](ADR-004.md)).
- **Phase 5:** Opportunity workspace, Collections decision management, transient Product Compare, and strict Saved Views.
- **Phase 6:** Manual-first VND Affiliate Economics scenarios, rational integer rounding, ceiling break-even calculations, and compatibility marker `"3"` ([`ADR-005`](ADR-005.md)).

Research conducted for Phase 7 ([`research/PHASE_7_CONTENT_INTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH.md`](../../research/PHASE_7_CONTENT_INTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH.md), accepted under [`PROJECT_CONTROL_PHASE7_RESEARCH_ACCEPTED.md`](../../PROJECT_CONTROL_PHASE7_RESEARCH_ACCEPTED.md)) investigated video ingestion options, reverse-engineering libraries, video velocity formulations, and database schema implications.

Key findings and architectural tensions:
1. **Third-Party Scraping Wrapper Fragility:** Unofficial API wrappers such as `davidteather/TikTok-Api` suffer from frequent endpoint breakage and signature invalidations (`msToken`, `_signature`, `X-Bogus`). A direct dependency on such libraries would introduce critical operational vulnerability into our local-first application.
2. **Reverse Index Absence:** TikTok Shop does not expose a public API mapping a `product_id` to all public TikTok videos that feature that product. Broad hashtag or keyword web search requires anti-bot bypass and is aggressively rate-limited.
3. **Attribution Disconnect:** Content views and creator engagement indicate viral spread and social momentum, but do *not* guarantee sales or affiliate commission. Blending video view velocity into the product `Trend Score` would distort objective market sales velocity.
4. **Storage & Resource Constraints:** Downloading, storing, or processing video media binaries (MP4 files) would rapidly consume operator disk space and bandwidth, conflicting with our lightweight desktop core.
5. **Disaster Recovery & Backup Compatibility:** Adding content tables requires additive schema creation and advances the portable backup compatibility marker under ADR-004 same-version restore invariants.

To resolve these tensions and establish a permanent architecture foundation, ADR-006 codifies domain separation, association modalities, ingestion mechanics, mathematical formulations, database schemas, backup compatibility, and query budgets.

## Decision

### 1. Domain Separation & Metric Independence
The architecture strictly enforces orthogonal separation across four distinct domains:

1. **Market Evidence Domain (Phases 1–3):**
   - Product cumulative sold count, 24-hour sales velocity, 7-day delta, sales acceleration, review velocity, rating score.
   - Authoritative `Trend Score` (0–100), trend confidence, trend state, and Why Trending explanations.
   - *Semantics:* Objective algorithmic observations of external market sales activity on TikTok Shop.
2. **User Economics Assumptions Domain (Phase 6):**
   - Assumed commission rate (`commission_rate_bps`), frozen price proxy (`commission_base_vnd`), fixed costs, variable costs, break-even orders.
   - *Semantics:* Explicit user-defined business scenario parameters.
3. **Content Intelligence Domain (Phase 7):**
   - Associated TikTok videos, creator identities (`uniqueId`, nickname), descriptions, published timestamps.
   - Time-series video engagement statistics: views (`playCount`), likes (`diggCount`), comments (`commentCount`), shares (`shareCount`), bookmarks (`collectCount`).
   - Derived content velocity: `views_velocity_24h`, `likes_velocity_24h`, `engagement_velocity`.
   - *Semantics:* Objective observations of creator video engagement associated with the product.
4. **Composite Opportunity Scoring (Deferred to Later Phase):**
   - Any unified composite score synthesizing Market Demand, Unit Economics, and Content Momentum is strictly deferred.

**Score Isolation Invariant:**
Content velocity metrics must NEVER alter, weigh into, or contaminate:
- Phase 2 `Trend Score` calculation;
- Trend confidence evaluation or trend state classification;
- Watchlist membership or monitoring criteria;
- Background crawl scheduling or alert triggers.

### 2. Ingestion & Association Architecture

#### 2.1 Association Modalities
Phase 7 implements two primary, resilient association modalities:
1. **User-Assisted URL Entry:** Operators input valid TikTok video URLs (`https://www.tiktok.com/@{username}/video/{video_id}`) directly on the Product Detail page.
2. **PDP Showcase Harvesting:** When crawling a product's TikTok Shop PDP, the collector harvests creator showcase video URLs (`/video/`) present in the rendered HTML/DOM.

Broad unguided hashtag search and automated anti-bot discovery bypasses are strictly out of scope.

#### 2.2 HTTP-First SSR Extraction
Video metadata and statistics are collected using our proven HTTP-first collector pattern:
1. Issue an asynchronous HTTP GET request with standard browser headers and `Accept-Language: vi-VN,vi;q=0.9`.
2. Extract the server-rendered `<script id="__UNIVERSAL_DATA_FOR_REHYDRATION__" type="application/json">` tag.
3. Parse the embedded JSON payload:
   `__DEFAULT_SCOPE__` → `webapp.video-detail` → `itemInfo` → `itemStruct` → `stats` and `author`.
4. Fall back to `SIGI_STATE` if `__UNIVERSAL_DATA_FOR_REHYDRATION__` is absent.
5. Fall back to headless Playwright session reuse only when a WAF challenge is encountered.

#### 2.3 Zero Media Binary Storage
- Under NO circumstances will video media binaries (.mp4, .webm) or raw audio streams be downloaded or saved to disk.
- Only scalar metadata (video ID, author handle, nickname, caption, view/like metrics) and public HTTPS thumbnail URLs are persisted.

### 3. Mathematical Velocity Formulations

#### 3.1 24h Views Velocity ($V_{\text{views}}$)
For a given video with latest snapshot $S_{\text{latest}}$ at $t_{\text{latest}}$:
Find a prior snapshot $S_{\text{prior}}$ at $t_{\text{prior}}$ within the bounded candidate window:
$$t_{\text{prior}} \in [t_{\text{latest}} - 28\text{h},\, t_{\text{latest}} - 20\text{h}]$$
If an eligible snapshot exists:
$$\Delta t = \frac{t_{\text{latest}} - t_{\text{prior}}}{86,400\text{ seconds}}$$
$$\Delta \text{views} = \text{play\_count}(t_{\text{latest}}) - \text{play\_count}(t_{\text{prior}})$$
$$V_{\text{views}} = \frac{\Delta \text{views}}{\Delta t}$$

#### 3.2 24h Likes Velocity ($V_{\text{likes}}$)
$$\Delta \text{likes} = \text{digg\_count}(t_{\text{latest}}) - \text{digg\_count}(t_{\text{prior}})$$
$$V_{\text{likes}} = \frac{\Delta \text{likes}}{\Delta t}$$

#### 3.3 Engagement Rate & Engagement Velocity
Total engagement $E(t) = \text{digg\_count} + \text{comment\_count} + \text{share\_count} + \text{collect\_count}$.
$$V_{\text{engagement}} = \frac{E(t_{\text{latest}}) - E(t_{\text{prior}})}{\Delta t}$$
$$\text{Engagement Rate} = \begin{cases}
\frac{E(t_{\text{latest}})}{\text{play\_count}(t_{\text{latest}})} & \text{if } \text{play\_count}(t_{\text{latest}}) > 0 \\
\text{null} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}$$

#### 3.4 Invariants & Null Safety
- **Null ≠ Zero:** Missing prior observations ($< 20\text{h}$ of history) strictly produce `null`, never `0`.
- **Non-Negative Delta Clamping:** If platform metric adjustments result in $\Delta < 0$, velocity is clamped to $0$ with an explanatory flag `anomaly_detected = True`.
- **Exact Rational Rounding:** All values are rounded using exact integer ratio round-half-up $(2N + D) // (2D)$ for whole-number displays.

### 4. Persistent Data Model & Concurrency

Phase 7 adds exactly two application-data tables to SQLite:

#### 4.1 Table: `product_videos`
Represents the durable association between a tracked Product and a TikTok video.
- `id`: Integer primary key.
- `product_id`: Integer foreign key referencing `products.id` (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
- `video_id`: String (VARCHAR(64)), TikTok's canonical numeric video ID.
- `video_url`: String (VARCHAR(512)), canonical video URL.
- `creator_handle`: String (VARCHAR(128)), author `uniqueId` (e.g. `@bangbangfood`).
- `creator_nickname`: String (VARCHAR(256)), author display name.
- `creator_avatar_url`: String (VARCHAR(512)), public avatar image URL.
- `description`: Text, video caption and hashtags.
- `thumbnail_url`: String (VARCHAR(512)), public thumbnail image URL.
- `published_at`: UTC timestamp, video creation time from TikTok.
- `source_type`: String (`user_entry` or `pdp_showcase`).
- `created_at`: UTC timestamp.
- `updated_at`: UTC timestamp.
- **Constraints & Indexes:**
  - `CONSTRAINT uq_product_videos_product_video UNIQUE (product_id, video_id)`
  - Index on `product_id`
  - Index on `video_id`

#### 4.2 Table: `video_snapshots`
Represents an immutable point-in-time observation of a video's engagement metrics.
- `id`: Integer primary key.
- `video_id`: Integer foreign key referencing `product_videos.id` (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
- `play_count`: BigInteger (nullable).
- `digg_count`: BigInteger (nullable).
- `comment_count`: BigInteger (nullable).
- `share_count`: BigInteger (nullable).
- `collect_count`: BigInteger (nullable).
- `captured_at`: UTC timestamp.
- `source_channel`: String (`http` or `playwright`).
- `is_available`: Boolean (default True, set False if video is removed or made private).
- **Indexes:**
  - Composite index `ix_video_snapshots_video_captured (video_id, captured_at DESC)`

#### 4.3 Additive Bootstrap
Schema creation proceeds strictly via SQLAlchemy `Base.metadata.create_all()`. Existing tables (`products`, `product_snapshots`, `watchlist_items`, `alert_rules`, `collections`, `economics_scenarios`) are unmodified.

### 5. Disaster Recovery & Backup Compatibility Version `"4"`

1. **Compatibility Advance:**
   - Under ADR-004 same-version restore invariants, adding `product_videos` and `video_snapshots` advances the portable backup compatibility marker from `"3"` to `"4"`.
   - `system_metadata` will store `portable_backup_compatibility_version = "4"`.
2. **Deterministic Rejection:**
   - Pre-restore candidate validation strictly rejects `v1`, `v2`, and `v3` backups with structured HTTP 400.
3. **Hot Backup Engine:**
   - `BACKUP.bat` will produce `tinix-affiliate-v4-*.sqlite3` online backups.
   - Pre-restore safety backups, rollback mechanisms, and Pre-Start Manifest Guard operate identically.
4. **Credential Isolation:**
   - Host-bound session files (`backend/.local/tiktok-session.json`) remain strictly isolated from all backups and exports.

---

### 6. Query Budgets & Zero N+1 Enforcement

1. **Product Detail Budget:**
   - Query 1: Fetch `Product` + snapshots (Phase 2 budget).
   - Query 2: Fetch default `EconomicsScenario` (Phase 6 budget).
   - Query 3: Fetch `ProductVideo` records with joined latest `VideoSnapshot`.
   - **Total Budget:** $\le 3$ SQL queries for full Product Detail page rendering.
2. **Collections & Compare Budget:**
   - If video counts or highlights are displayed in Collections, batched queries `WHERE product_id IN (...)` must be used.
   - Strictly 0 additional queries per row (Zero N+1).
3. **Latency Hard Gate:**
   - All detail views must render in $< 20\text{ms}$ on a 200-product / 1,000-video / 10,000-snapshot benchmark dataset (hard gate: $< 200\text{ms}$).

---

### 7. Transparency & Commercial Disclaimers

Every user-facing interface that displays video velocity or engagement statistics must feature a visible commercial disclaimer:
> *"Dữ liệu video được thu thập công khai từ TikTok. Tốc độ xem và tương tác phản ánh mức độ lan tỏa nội dung, không đại diện trực tiếp cho doanh số thực tế của nhà sáng tạo."*

## Consequences

### Positive
- **Complete Decision Triangle:** Delivers the third foundational pillar of the project vision (`Product Data + Affiliate Economics + Content Signals`).
- **Resilient & Low-Maintenance:** Eliminates dependency on fragile third-party scrapers (`TikTok-Api`) by relying on native HTTP-first SSR parsing.
- **Zero Storage Bloat:** Zero local video files; SQLite storage footprint grows by mere kilobytes per video.
- **Deterministic Performance:** Enforces strict query budgets ($\le 3$ SQL queries) and zero N+1 queries.
- **Disaster Recovery Safety:** Preserves ADR-004 same-version restore guarantees with compatibility version `"4"`.

### Negative / Trade-offs
- **User-Assisted Linking Requirement:** Automated video discovery is limited to PDP showcase links; discovering arbitrary videos tagged with a product requires operator URL entry.
- **WAF Sensitivity:** High-frequency querying of TikTok video URLs may encounter WAF challenges, requiring Playwright session reuse or cooldown delays.

### Mitigations
- Operator session reuse via `tiktok-session.json` minimizes challenge frequency.
- Explicit `is_available = False` handles deleted or private videos gracefully without corrupting database integrity.
